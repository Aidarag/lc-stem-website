Robeson County is one of the most medically underserved counties in North
Carolina. When we examined the data, the pattern was consistent. Residents were
not avoiding care because they did not want it. They were avoiding it because
the distance, the transportation, and the time cost of reaching a clinic made
routine care impractical.

Our solution, Bringing the Clinic to the House, reverses that equation. Rather
than asking patients to close the distance, we designed an approach that
delivers care into the communities where people already live, targeting the
specific access gaps the county data revealed.

The work was produced for the 2026 Mastercard Data Challenge, hosted by the AUC
Data Science Initiative, where our team was one of those selected from 106
competing HBCU teams.
