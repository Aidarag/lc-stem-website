**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**  
**Livingstone College STEM Website**

**Vision**  
Every year, Livingstone College STEM students accomplish incredible things. They build  
innovative projects, earn internships, conduct research, compete in hackathons, receive  
scholarships, and win academic awards. Unfortunately, many of these achievements are  
only shared within the campus community or on social media, making it difficult for  
prospective students, parents, employers, and partners to see the impact of the STEM  
Department.  
The Livingstone College STEM Website will become the department's official digital  
showcase—a place where stories, achievements, and opportunities are brought together  
in one modern, inspiring platform.  
Rather than simply presenting information, the website will tell the story of the  
students, faculty, and community that make Livingstone STEM exceptional.

**Product Goal**  
Product Requirements Document (PRD) 1Create a modern, responsive website that strengthens the online presence of the  
Livingstone College STEM Department while celebrating its people and  
accomplishments.  
To ensure a successful launch, development will be completed in **three phases**,  
beginning with a simple informational website focused entirely on student success.

**Phase 1 — Student Showcase (MVP)**  
**Objective**  
Build a clean, engaging website that highlights the achievements of Livingstone College  
STEM students.  
This phase is **100% informational**. Visitors can explore content, but there are **no**  
**student accounts, logins, or dashboards**. All content is managed by department  
administrators.

**Target Audience**  
Prospective students  
Current students  
Parents and families  
Alumni  
Employers  
Community members
